# Yahtzee Rules

## Objective

Score as many points as possible over **13 turns**. Each turn you roll five dice and
score exactly one category. The player with the highest total score wins.

## Turn Flow

1. **Roll** all five dice (or any subset not held from a previous roll).
2. Optionally **hold** any dice you want to keep, then roll the remaining dice again.
3. You may roll a **third time**, again keeping any dice you choose.
4. After up to three rolls, **score exactly one unscored category** from the scorecard.

> You must score a category every turn — even if no category fits well, you must
> enter **0** in one of them.

## Upper Section

Score the **sum of the matching face value** for each category.

- **Ones** — sum of all dice showing 1
- **Twos** — sum of all dice showing 2
- **Threes** — sum of all dice showing 3
- **Fours** — sum of all dice showing 4
- **Fives** — sum of all dice showing 5
- **Sixes** — sum of all dice showing 6

### Upper Bonus

If your Upper Section total is **63 or more**, you earn a **+35 bonus**.
(Scoring three of each number 1–6 adds up to exactly 63.)

## Lower Section

- **Three of a Kind** — at least three dice show the same face; score is the **sum of all five dice**
- **Four of a Kind** — at least four dice show the same face; score is the **sum of all five dice**
- **Full House** — three of one number and two of another; scores **25 points**
- **Small Straight** — four sequential dice (e.g. 1-2-3-4 or 3-4-5-6); scores **30 points**
- **Large Straight** — five sequential dice (e.g. 1-2-3-4-5 or 2-3-4-5-6); scores **40 points**
- **Yahtzee** — all five dice show the same face; scores **50 points**
- **Chance** — any combination; score is the **sum of all five dice** (no minimum requirement)

## Scoring a Zero

You may score **any unscored category as 0** at any time during your turn
(even after the first roll). This is useful when you cannot form a desired
combination and prefer to preserve other categories for later turns.

Simply select an empty category without meeting its criteria — it will be
recorded as **0 points**.
